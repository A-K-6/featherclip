Copy. Choose. Carry on.

A small popup.
A familiar rhythm.

01

Copy something. Text, a picture, or files. FeatherClip remembers new copies while it’s running.

02

Open your history. Use ⌘⇧V on Mac or Ctrl+Alt+V on X11. On Wayland, set a shortcut for featherclip --toggle.

03

Choose and paste. Click a clip or press Enter. Use Shift+Enter to copy without pasting. Enable direct paste on Mac.
